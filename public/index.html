Sign in with Facebook and Instagram
First-time setup (5 minutes) and required permissions
  1. developers.facebook.com → your app → App settings → Basic: copy App ID and App Secret here and click Save.
  2. Under Add product, add Facebook Login (or Facebook Login for Business) → Settings → paste the redirect URL into Valid OAuth Redirect URIs → Save.
  3. Click Sign in with Facebook and Instagram, choose your page and Instagram account, and approve all permissions.
  4. Then click Check Instagram & comments — every line should show a check mark.
Note: while the app is in Development mode, permissions only work for admin accounts. For the bot to reply to everyone, Meta App Review is required for messaging and comment permissions.

AI answering on your phone number

Calls to your number are forwarded to a Twilio number. The assistant answers by voice from the company files, books the engineer meeting, and transfers the caller to your phone when they ask for a person. Every call is saved to customers and Google Sheets.
In the recommended mode this can be your own number.
